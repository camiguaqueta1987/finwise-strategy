# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 1 · Value Creation. Reaching $10M isn’t enough if users aren’t experiencing FinWise’s value. FinWise is spending heavily on acquisition while failing to convert trial users into paying customers or retain them over time. Only 2% of trial users convert, and 40% are retained after one year.

**Model:** Freemium. It builds a large pool of user data, behavioral insights, low CAC and strong network effects, rather than a highly predictable upsell pool. With a reverse-trial model, the challenge is deciding which features remain free while ensuring users experience enough value to stay engaged and recognize the additional benefits of upgrading to Premium.

_____

## Recommendation

FinWise should stay with the reverse trial + freemium model for now. It's not the time to change, because FinWise first needs to improve how users understand the product's value. Once that's solved, it can invest extra resources in testing a pricing change.

**The pricing bet:** We believe that offering full Premium access during the reverse trial, followed by a limited free plan, will increase activation and willingness to pay by allowing users to experience the value of automation and collaboration before committing to a subscription.

_____

## Model signal

Bank connection completion rate, invoice creation completion rate, automation completion rate and dashboard views. Our priority is to drive activation by allowing users to experience FinWise's full value during the reverse trial. After the trial, essential features and historical transactions remain free, while premium capabilities such as automatic bank synchronization, advanced reporting, and collaboration require a paid subscription.
