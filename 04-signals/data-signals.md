## The data pattern

Import Data usage varies substantially, from 30.51% to 45.42%, while Trial-to-Paid conversion remains relatively stable, between 1.87% and 2.08%. The Pearson correlation coefficient is approximately −0.05, indicating virtually no linear correlation between the two metrics at the monthly level.

Pattern classification: Import Data shows fluctuations, while Trial-to-Paid conversion shows a Ceiling around 2%.

_Metric patterns: Conversion, Ceiling - Consistently around 2% across 13 months. Modeling usage, Cliff - Sudden drop between September and October 2024. Import usage, Pattern - Moves between approximately 30% and 45%, with no sustained upward or downward trend. Session duration, Cliff - Drops from 12.11 minutes in June 2024 to 6.99 minutes in July (−42.3%). Sessions/user, Pattern - Declines from 9 to 3, then recovers to 9._
